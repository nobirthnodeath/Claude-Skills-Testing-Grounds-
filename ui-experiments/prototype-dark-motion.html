Prototype · throwaway · Round 7 · Graphite only

Graphite with a living background

A WebGL shader runs behind each screen. It warps flowing noise, then samples it three times, once each for red, green and blue, at slightly different offsets. That split makes the color fringes, like a lens with chromatic aberration. The cards and the tab bar are frosted glass, so the motion shows softly through them. Use the bar at the bottom to change the look, the strength and the motion.
